# One-Hour Internship Orientation — Session Guide

Use this as a flexible agenda; adjust times if you have more than one speaker.

**PPT storyline:** Company & achievements → Departments (projects) → Hands-on expertise → Internship logistics → Q&A.

---

## Agenda Slide Content

**Slide title:** Orientation Session Agenda

Use this as the main agenda slide:

1. **Introduction to the REUDE Team & Mentors**  
   Meet the leadership team, department leads, mentors, and the people who will guide interns during the program.

2. **Overview of Internship Scope & Project Tracks**  
   Understand the internship structure, available project areas, expected learning outcomes, and how students will be mapped to teams.

3. **Interaction with Our Diverse Team**  
   Get introduced to work happening across **AI, drones, hardware, deeptech, and business**, and see how different teams collaborate on real products.

4. **Q&A — Ask Us Anything**  
   Open discussion for students to clarify project expectations, tools, timelines, mentorship, and career learning opportunities.

**Speaker opening line:**  
"Today’s session is designed to help you understand REUDE, meet the people behind the work, explore the internship tracks, and ask anything you need before getting started."

---

## Before the Session (5 Minutes of Your Prep)

- Confirm room/link, slides, and who introduces whom.
- Align each department lead on the **same slide template** (see below) and time limit.
- Have a one-pager ready: start date, hours, main contact, tools/links, first-week expectations.
- Test screen share and mic if remote.

---

## Recommended Session Order (Matches Your PPT)

Tell the story top-down: **why we exist → what we build → what you will do → how you will work here.**

| Order | Block | Time | Purpose |
| ------- | -------- | ------ | --------- |
| **1** | Welcome & session goals | 3 min | Set tone; explain agenda and Q&A rules. |
| **2** | Company overview & achievements | 8 min | Credibility, mission, products, milestones, awards, customers/partners (high level). |
| **3** | Org map (one slide) | 2 min | Show how departments connect before deep dives. |
| **4** | Department intros & projects | 28 min | Per department: role → completed work → ongoing work → where interns fit (1 line). |
| **5** | Hands-on expertise for interns | 10 min | Skills, tools, and outcomes mapped to departments/projects they just heard. |
| **6** | People, tools & expectations | 6 min | Mentors, channels, hours, conduct, security, first-week checklist. |
| **7** | Q&A & next steps | 3 min | One clear action (e.g. read onboarding doc, book buddy 1:1). |

**Total:** ~60 minutes (stretch to 75–90 min if you have more than 4–5 departments).

### Why this order works

- **Company first** — Students understand *why* before *what*.
- **Departments before expertise** — They connect skills to real projects they just saw.
- **Expertise before logistics** — Motivation stays high; admin comes after the “why join” story.
- **Logistics last** — Practical details are remembered when they know where they might land.

## PPT Slide Order (Suggested)

| Section | Slides (suggested) |
| --------- | ------------------- |
| **Opening** | Title, agenda, house rules (confidentiality, questions welcome). |
| **Company** | Mission/vision, products/services, timeline, achievements, clients/partners (optional). |
| **Org** | Single org chart or “how we work together” diagram. |
| **Per department** (repeat) | Dept name + lead → what the team does → **completed projects** (2–3) → **ongoing projects** (2–3) → intern touchpoint (optional 1 slide). |
| **Hands-on** | Matrix: department × skills/tools × example intern tasks/outcomes. |
| **Internship** | Program structure, mentors, tools (repo/chat/board), evaluation/feedback, first week. |
| **Close** | Contacts, resources/links, thank you, Q&A. |

### Per-department template (keep every team consistent)

Use the same structure for each presenter (~5–7 min per department):

1. **Who we are** — One-line purpose of the department.
2. **What we’ve delivered** — 2–3 completed projects (outcome, not only title).
3. **What we’re building now** — 2–3 ongoing projects (status: prototype / pilot / production).
4. **How interns engage** — Example tasks or skills (feeds the next “hands-on” section).

**Department presentation order (recommended):**

Follow the **product lifecycle** so the story flows naturally:

1. R&D / Hardware / Mechanical  
2. Embedded / Firmware / Electronics  
3. Software / AI / Data  
4. Testing / QA / Validation  
5. Operations / Business / Marketing (if interns rotate there)

If interns are **assigned to one department**, put that department **second** (right after company overview) for relevance, then cover the rest briefly.

## Hands-On Expertise Section (What to Cover)

Present this **after** all department talks, as one consolidated block:

| Element | What to say |
| --------- | ------------- |
| **Skills** | Languages, frameworks, CAD, simulation, lab equipment, etc. |
| **Tools** | Git, Jira/Notion, MATLAB, Jetson, sensors, test benches — whatever you actually use. |
| **Outcomes** | What they can put on a resume or portfolio (e.g. “deployed module,” “test report,” “demo”). |
| **Mapping** | One table: *Department → Project type → Skills you’ll practice*. |

Optional: one **live demo** (repo, board, or hardware) in this block instead of extra slides.

## Suggested 60-Minute Flow (Detailed)

| Time | Topic | What to Cover |
| ------ | -------- | ---------------- |
| **0–3 min** | Welcome | Host intro, agenda, expectations for the hour. |
| **3–11 min** | Company & achievements | Mission, products, milestones, awards, impact. |
| **11–13 min** | Org map | How departments fit together. |
| **13–41 min** | Departments | Each dept: intro, completed projects, ongoing projects, intern hook. |
| **41–51 min** | Hands-on expertise | Skills, tools, outcomes; link back to projects just shown. |
| **51–57 min** | Logistics & first week | Mentors, contacts, tools access, hours, conduct, day-one checklist. |
| **57–60 min** | Q&A + next step | Single follow-up action. |

---

## What to Emphasize (Short List)

1. **Clarity** — One sentence on how they get unblocked (person + channel).
2. **Psychological safety** — Questions are expected; no “stupid” questions in week one.
3. **Ownership** — Small scope, clear deliverable, visible progress (even drafts).
4. **Feedback** — When and how they’ll get it (e.g. weekly 1:1, code review norms).

---

## Optional Materials to Share After

- Onboarding checklist (accounts, reading, first task).
- Link tree: handbook, calendar, handbook/legal, IT help.
- Glossary of acronyms your team uses.

---

## Facilitation Tips

- **Two voices** if possible (HR + tech lead): keeps energy and splits expertise.
- **One live demo** beats many slides (e.g. open the board or repo and show one real task).
- **End with a concrete “do this by EOD”** so the hour converts to action.

---

## Customization Notes

Tailor this guide for your setting:

- **Startup vs corporate** — Adjust depth on policies and compliance.
- **Remote vs on-site** — Add logistics for access, equipment pickup, or timezone norms.
- **Engineering vs mixed roles** — Swap the “How we work” demo (repo/board vs design tools).

Consider adding a minute-by-minute script and slide titles once your audience and format are fixed.
