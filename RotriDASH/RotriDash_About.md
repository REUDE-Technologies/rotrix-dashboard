# About RotriDash

**RotriDash** is the motor assessment workspace for teams that run propulsion tests on thrust beds and dynos.

---

RotriDash is a motor assessment workspace built for teams that run propulsion tests on thrust beds and dynos. It ingests standard bench exports and turns them into clear, comparable results—so you keep precision and traceability without stitching together spreadsheets and one-off scripts.

The dashboard brings thrust, torque, RPM, electrical power, efficiency, and time-based behaviour into one place. Configure dual-axis plots, compare multiple files, summarise throttle bands, and produce presentation-ready PDF reports from a single streamlined interface.

Built for drone motor developers, EV drivetrain teams, high-payload and hobby UAV programs, commercial test labs, academic research, and defence qualification workflows. RotriDash is designed to help you make concrete, data-driven powertrain decisions for your next design, batch, or certification step.
